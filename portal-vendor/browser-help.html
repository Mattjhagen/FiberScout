Open in Safari

On iPhone or iPad, this Wi-Fi pop-up may block Messages and Contacts. FiberScout cannot reliably open Safari automatically. If you are already in Safari, use the actions below.

  1. Press and hold the address below and choose Copy, or type it yourself.
  2. Leave this pop-up and open the Safari app. Keep connected to Try T-Mobile Fiber. If offered, choose Use Without Internet. Cancel may disconnect Wi-Fi; reconnect in Settings if needed.
  3. Paste or type the address into Safari's address bar and tap Go. Use http://, not https://.

The home page is http://192.168.4.1. If a Share menu offers Open in Safari, you can try it; otherwise use the address above. A link in this pop-up may stay in this pop-up.

Safari text & contact actions

In regular Safari, this page offers iPhone text links (including each plan) and a contact card link. Tap Text Me or Select Plan to prepare a message, or Add to Contacts to preview the card and save it. Nothing is sent automatically.
